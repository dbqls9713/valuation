### FY2026 Q1 (2026-04-29 보고)

**Headline 정량**: 매출 $109.9B (+22% YoY, 11분기 연속 두 자리 성장) · Cloud +63% ($20B+) · Cloud RPO $462B (QoQ 거의 2배) · Waymo 50만/주 (1년 만에 2배). 출처: [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/29/alphabet-googl-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/), [Pichai blog](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q1-2026/).

#### Pichai prepared remarks (핵심 자기 framing)

- **Compute constraint 직접 인정**: "We are compute constrained in the near term…**our cloud revenue would have been higher if you were able to meet the demand**." → **#14 나쁜 일에도 소통** 신호. 매출 상한이 capacity인 점을 표면화. (📌 형용사 후보: **솔직한 / 약점도 표면화하는**)
- **효율 자랑 (Gemini 3 가성비)**: "Since upgrading AI overviews and AI mode to Gemini 3, **we've reduced the cost of core AI responses by more than 30%**." (📌 형용사 후보: **효율을 측정·공개하는**)
- **풀스택 차별화**: "Our custom TPUs, Axion CPUs and the latest NVIDIA GPUs continue to form **the industry's widest variety of compute options**." (📌 형용사 후보: **버티컬 풀스택을 자기 정체성으로 두는**)

#### Q&A 핵심 발췌

##### Brian Nowak (Morgan Stanley) — TPU pricing & ROIC 접근

> Q: What are the areas that you are most excited about applying next-generation compute toward to sort of generate an ROIC on that return in search in the next 12 months?
>
> Pichai: "I see bringing agentic flows, workflows to consumers…as a huge opportunity ahead…our investments in our full stack of AI approach…puts us in a good position."
>
> Pichai (TPU pricing): "We do think about it as what are we doing through Google Cloud to help our customers…we are opportunistic about it. But…**we do take ROIC approach**."

##### Doug Anmuth (JPMorgan) — 2027 CAPEX 궤적

> Q: How do you think about the current CapEx trajectory, the ability to service this massive backlog?
>
> Ashkenazi: "We have done it very thoughtfully to meet the demand…You're seeing the proof point, the ROIC on that…the growth rate we're seeing…**We'll provide more clarity in future earnings call**."

##### Philipp Schindler — Search query coverage 상승 여지

> Q: How much room there may be to increase coverage of search queries?
>
> A: "With the ability of AI to better understand intent…I think there is upside in that coverage number…our ability to deliver ads on longer, more complex searches."

##### Eric Sheridan (Goldman Sachs) — 경쟁 위치

> Q: How that positions you…competitively in your mind relative to others?
>
> Pichai: "We are genuinely differentiated…unique in the market because of our **vertically optimized AI stack**…we own frontier models, own the silicon…**we are the only provider**…that offers all of these vertical stack."

##### Michael Nathanson — capacity 우선순위

> Q: How are you deciding how you allocating which divisions get excess capacity?
>
> Pichai: "The foundation where we start with it is **what we need from a R&D standpoint to develop models at the frontier**…compute needed for [model development] because it's a foundation for everything."

##### Anat Ashkenazi — Cloud margin 확장 driver

> Q: What's driving that margin expansion [in Cloud]?
>
> A: "The robust strong revenue growth…provides leverage all the way down to the bottom line…we've been working hard to ensure we have…a productive and efficient organization…**we are looking at how we drive scientific process innovation**."

##### Ken Gawrelski (Wells Fargo) — Subscription vs ad 모델

> Q: Do you see future use cases where certain consumer use cases are more effectively monetized via subscriptions?
>
> Pichai: "There are going to be use cases where people will want to use the most powerful model…**we're going to put the user first**…we already provide various tiers of our subscription plans."

##### Justin Post (BofA) — TPU vs GCP backlog 분해

> Q: How much break down the backlog growth a little bit between TPUs and cloud?
>
> Ashkenazi: "The TPU hardware agreements…are reflected in our cloud backlog of the $462 billion…**Although the majority of the backlog is still GCP agreements**…just over half of it will convert to revenue in the next 24 months."
>

### FY2025 Q4 (2026-02-04 보고)

**Headline 정량**: FY2025 매출 $403B (+15%), Q4 $113.8B (+18%) · 순익 $34.5B (+30%) · OCF $52.4B (분기 record), FCF $24.6B · Cloud +48% ($17.7B) · Cloud RPO **$240B** (QoQ +55%, YoY 2배+) · **2026 CAPEX 가이던스 $175-185B** · Waymo 누적 2천만회/주당 40만회/마이애미 진출. 출처: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/04/alphabet-googl-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/), [SEC release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000012/googexhibit991q42025.htm).

#### Pichai prepared remarks (핵심 자기 framing)

- **Gemini 3 효율 자랑 (78% 단가 절감)**: "**Lower Gemini serving unit costs by 78% over 2025** through model optimizations, efficiency, and utilization improvements." (📌 형용사 후보: **효율을 측정·공개하는 / 단가 압축 트랙 record가 있는**)
- **2026 CAPEX 큰 폭 상향 ($91B → $175-185B)**: "$175 billion to $185 billion range, primarily to support AI compute, technical infrastructure, and cloud growth." (📌 형용사 후보: **자기 컨빅션에 자본 베팅하는 — 단 ROI 검증은 2-3년 후**)
- **Cloud 백로그 가속**: "$240 billion, up 55% sequentially and more than double year over year." (다음 분기 Q1 2026엔 $462B로 또 2배)

#### Q&A 핵심 발췌

##### Brian Nowak (Morgan Stanley) — Agentic commerce

> Q: Most progress on new types of agentic commerce products?
>
> Pichai: "I definitely think '25 was more about laying the foundation getting the models to start being more robust in agentic use cases…the launch of universal Commerce protocol at NRF in January with a bunch of partners…has been super well received."

##### Eric Sheridan (Goldman Sachs) — Compute capacity 격차 + 효율

> Q: With the step function change in CapEx for 2026, can you talk about pathway to closing compute gaps?
>
> Pichai: "I expect the demand…is exceptionally strong. And so I do **to go through the year in a supply constrained way**."
>
> Ashkenazi: "We don't view this as **an episodic one time project**…but rather how we run the business on a regular basis." (효율은 일회성 프로젝트 아니라 운영 방식으로 자리매김)

##### Doug Anmuth (JPMorgan) — Gemini 위치 + TPU 외부 판매

> Q: How can Google build/maintain Gemini position…how should we think about TPUs moving outside Google Cloud?
>
> Pichai: "We're obviously improving these models across many paradigms…I think we are in a **very, very relentless innovation cadence**." (📌 형용사 후보: **집요한 / 끈질긴 / 한 페이스를 유지하는**)

##### Mark Mahaney (Evercore) — YouTube 광고 +9% / SaaS economics

> Schindler: "**For the full year 2025, our YouTube's annual revenue surpassed $60 billion** across ads and subscription."
>
> Pichai (SaaS 침식 우려에): "The successful companies…are definitely incorporating Gemini deeply in critical workflows…it is **an enabling tool**." (자기 입장은: 대체재 아니라 enable 도구라고 framing)

##### Michael Nathanson — Universal Commerce Protocol + CAPEX 구성

> Pichai: "People go through a lot of commercial journeys across many of our surfaces…I think there's…a huge foundational uplift here."
>
> Ashkenazi (CAPEX 구성): "**Approximately 60% of our investment in 2025 and it's going to be fairly similar in 2026, went towards machines**." (40% data center·networking)

##### Ross Sandler (Barclays) — Gemini App 사용량·잔존

> Pichai: "There was a sharp increase in engagement per user on the app…All the metrics be it active usage, the intensity of usage, retention, all showed distinct progress."

##### Ken Gawrelski (Wells Fargo) — AI Search monetization 진화

> Schindler: "The acceleration we saw in the search **was not due to a single driver, but was really the result of many different parts of our business showing strength**." (한 가지 magic 부정, 다축으로 누적 — 📌 형용사 후보: **단일 narrative를 거부하는**)

##### Justin Post (BofA) — Gemini app vs Search cannibalization

> Q: Are you seeing any cannibalization of search?
>
> Pichai: "I think we are giving people choice…the combination of all of that…creates an expansionary moment…**we haven't seen any evidence of cannibalization**."
>
> Schindler: "**We're not rushing anything here** [on Gemini app ads]." (📌 형용사 후보: **신중한 / 새 매출 도입에 deliberate한**)
>

#### Q&A 핵심 발췌

> Q: What factors are you most focused on to ensure a smooth transition for your search business as you move to a more agentic world?

> Schindler: "Agentic Experiences help us answer people's tough questions… **we need to integrate different partner ecosystems in a way that creates value**." (자기 답이 partner 통합이라는 framing)

##### Doug Anmuth (JPMorgan) — AI Overview economics (가장 중요한 Q&A)

##### Mark Shmulik (Bernstein) — Search economics 진화

> Pichai: "We are the only hyperscaler really building offerings on our own models and are **highly differentiated on our own technology**."

##### Justin Post (BofA) — Cloud 대형 deal economics

### FY2025 Q2 (2025-07-23 보고)

**Headline 정량**: 매출 $96.4B (+14%) · CAPEX 2025 가이던스 **$75B → $85B 상향** (이번 분기 $22.4B, 2/3 server 1/3 DC·networking) · OpEx +20% (그중 **$1.4B 법적 합의 charge**) · Cloud RPO $106B (QoQ +18%, YoY +38%) · Gemini 월 4.5억 MAU + **월 980조 토큰 처리**. 출처: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/07/23/alphabet-googl-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript/).

#### Pichai prepared remarks (핵심 자기 framing)

- **Compute supply 부족 사전 경고 (Q3·Q4·Q1 연속 일관)**: "We are seeing strong momentum…it's a tight supply environment…there is obviously a time delay between this additional investment will play out in future years." (📌 형용사 후보: **공급 격차를 사전 경고하는**)
- **2026 agent 본격화 예고**: "We are making robust progress…**expect 2026 to be the year in which people use agent experiences more broadly**."

#### Q&A 핵심 발췌

##### Eric Sheridan (Goldman Sachs) — 소비자 행동 변화 + monetization

> Pichai: "People are excited about AI…we expect as we build out our organic experiences, we have a good understanding of how to continue training on monetization…**we'll lead with organic experience**." (📌 organic 사용자 경험 우선, monetization은 후. 광고 욕심 자제 신호)

##### Doug Anmuth (JPMorgan) — Compute access + paid clicks

> Pichai: "We are seeing strong momentum…it's a tight supply environment…there is obviously **a time delay between this additional investment will play out in future years**." (CAPEX → 매출 delay 인정)
>
> Schindler: "**We don't manage to pay clicks targets**…paid clicks were up 4% year on year, but a number of factors affect these metrics…avoid drawing overly broad conclusions solely based on these metrics." (단일 KPI 매니징 부정)

##### Brian Nowak (Morgan Stanley) — Agentic 기술 hurdle

> Pichai: "Being able to do it reliably…**latency compounds, cost compounds**…we are making robust progress…expect 2026 to be the year in which people use agent experiences more broadly." (📌 형용사 후보: **시스템 제약을 정량 표현하는 / 엔지니어링 frame**)

##### Ross Sandler (Barclays) — AI Overviews CTR + OpenAI

> Schindler: "**AI overviews continue to drive higher satisfaction**…we see monetization at approximately the same rate, which gives us a really strong base to innovate and drive next-generation ad formats." (Q3와 동일 framing)

##### Mark Mahaney (Evercore) — 광고 환경 + Search/Gemini app 2-surface 전략

> Pichai: "Between these two surfaces, you're covering entire breadth and depth…**search is information-focused…Gemini app is more your personal assistant**…over time we can understand user intent better and abstract complexity." (2-surface 전략 명확)

##### Ken Gawrelski (Wells Fargo) — Cloud supply 완화 시점 + Agent web 집중도

> Q: 작년에 supply constraint이 2025 말 완화 예상이라 했는데 이제 2026 얘기, clarify?
>
> Ashkenazi: "We're working hard to bring more capacity online…more of an increase towards back end of year…**don't think about this in linear fashion**…quarter-to-quarter growth rates could depend on timing of capacity delivery." (📌 supply 완화 일정 후퇴 — 솔직 인정 + linear 사고 거부)
>
> Pichai (agent 집중): "I do think it's an opportunity for some players…**not just technology claim, but we have to solve business models for varying players involved**."

#### 톤·신호

- **법적 합의 $1.4B charge 솔직 표면화** (회피 없음)
- **Supply 완화 일정 1년 미루는 candid admission** — Q1 2024부터 "2025 말 완화" 했는데 Q2 2025에 "2026" 으로 후퇴
- **단일 KPI (paid clicks) 매니징 거부** — short-term metric narrative 거부
